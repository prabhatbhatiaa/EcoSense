Project Overview

Intelligent conservation through real-time environmental analysis.

Connecting agriculture with urban awareness.

The Soil Moisture Detecting System is a comprehensive IoT solution engineered to combat water wastage and monitor environmental health. By utilizing an Arduino UNO as the core processing unit, the system continuously reads data from integrated soil moisture and air quality sensors.

When moisture drops below a critical threshold, the smart irrigation logic automatically engages, conserving water while ensuring optimal hydration. Simultaneously, real-time AQI monitoring provides crucial alerts for indoor and outdoor environments, displayed directly on an onboard LCD and web dashboard.

Why it matters: Traditional irrigation wastes up to 50% of water due to inefficiencies. Our automated logic cuts this waste to near zero by reacting strictly to data.

Smart Irrigation Logic

Automated water pump activation based on real-time soil hydration metrics, preventing both over-watering and drought stress. The system learns the dry-out curve of the soil environment.

Environmental Telemetry

Continuous sampling of ambient air quality, providing an early warning system for high particulate and pollutant levels. Integrates MQ-135 readings for broad-spectrum gas detection.

How It Works

A seamless pipeline from physical detection to automated action.

01

Detection

Soil Moisture and AQI sensors continuously capture analog environmental data at 5-second intervals.

02

Processing

Arduino UNO converts the raw voltage inputs, mapping them to actionable percentage thresholds.

03

Optimization

Embedded algorithms determine precise water requirements based on historical dry-out rates.

04

Automation

5V Relays trigger physical mechanical systems, activating the submersible pump exactly when needed.

05

Display

Live metrics and hardware state alerts are pushed to the LCD interface and local digital dashboard.

Agricultural Impact

Driving sustainability in modern farming practices.

In large-scale agricultural setups, improper irrigation accounts for massive fresh-water waste, while poor air quality stunts crop growth. This system acts as an autonomous guardian for delicate ecosystems.

  • Prevents Wastage: Delivers precise milliliters of water strictly when root zones require it, bypassing scheduled human error.
  • Drought Resilience: Crucial for arid regions requiring strict water rationing and compliance tracking.
  • Crop Yield Optimization: Maintains perfect hydration curves, drastically reducing plant stress and increasing harvest weights.
  • Labor Efficiency: Eliminates the need for manual field moisture probing across acres of land.
